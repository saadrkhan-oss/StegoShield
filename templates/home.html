Core Capabilities

Enterprise-Grade Security Tools

Three integrated modules working together to secure, analyze, and monitor image-based data threats.

Steganographic Encryption

Embed secret files inside carrier images using LSB steganography with AES-256-GCM authenticated encryption and PBKDF2 key derivation (100,000 iterations).

Forensic Threat Scanner

Analyze any image through a 10-layer pipeline including YARA signatures, ClamAV, AI prediction, entropy mapping, and statistical steganalysis.

Intelligence Dashboard

SOC-grade real-time monitoring with threat distribution charts, scan history, risk classification, and full forensic report access for administrators.

Secure Extraction

Decrypt and extract hidden payloads from stego images with automatic threat scanning of extracted content before delivery.

Role-Based Access

Granular RBAC system separating Admin and Standard User privileges. Full forensic breakdown restricted to authorized security analysts.

AI Risk Scoring

Machine learning model trained on steganographic datasets predicts threat probability and assigns a 0-100 risk score with confidence metrics.
